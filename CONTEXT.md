# Σπίτι μου 3 Guide

An unofficial, informational tool that tells a prospective borrower whether they appear to qualify for the Greek state housing-loan programme Σπίτι μου 3, and roughly what the loan would cost.

## Language

### Programme

**Σπίτι μου 3**:
The Greek state housing-loan programme this tool is about. Announced, not yet launched; its terms are preliminary.
_Avoid_: Spiti mou III, the programme (when ambiguous with an earlier one)

**Σπίτι μου 2**:
The closed predecessor programme. Its published terms are the source of Assumptions for Σπίτι μου 3.
_Avoid_: previous plan

**ΚΥΑ**:
The ministerial decision that makes a programme's terms official. None exists yet for Σπίτι μου 3.
_Avoid_: law, regulation

### Applicants

**Αιτών**:
A person applying for the loan; a Household has one or two.
_Avoid_: borrower, user (in rule language)

**Νοικοκυριό**:
The one or two Αιτούντες applying together, assessed on a single family income.
_Avoid_: family, couple

**Οικογενειακό εισόδημα**:
The Household's annual income as shown on the tax return (εκκαθαριστικό), summed across spouses or partners.
_Avoid_: salary, gross income

### Property and loan

**Ακίνητο**:
The home being bought with the loan.
_Avoid_: house, flat

**Συμβατική αξία**:
The purchase price stated in the contract, against which the property cap and loan share are measured.
_Avoid_: price, market value

**Ίδια συμμετοχή**:
The part of the Συμβατική αξία the Αιτών pays from their own funds.
_Avoid_: down payment, deposit

**Άτοκο τμήμα**:
The interest-free, State-funded half of the loan.
_Avoid_: free half, subsidy

**Τραπεζικό τμήμα**:
The interest-bearing half of the loan, provided by a bank.
_Avoid_: bank loan

### Rules and results

**Κριτήριο**:
One condition of the programme (e.g. age range, income cap) that a Household either meets or fails.
_Avoid_: requirement, rule

**Κατάσταση κριτηρίου**:
How firmly a Κριτήριο is known: **Αναφερόμενο** (reported for Σπίτι μου 3), **Παραδοχή** (carried over from Σπίτι μου 2), or **Άγνωστο** (no data, shown but not evaluated).
_Avoid_: confidence, certainty

**Παραδοχή**:
A Κριτήριο or value assumed for Σπίτι μου 3 from Σπίτι μου 2 because Σπίτι μου 3 has not published its own.
_Avoid_: guess, default

**Φαίνεται κατάλληλος**:
The verdict that no Αναφερόμενο Κριτήριο fails. Never a guarantee; the bank and the official platform decide.
_Avoid_: eligible, approved, qualified
